## 1. The stale snapshot: grow `Win` to `Win′` and never edit the complement

**Acts on:**
- **(a) extends R3-R1-5 #2** (`Dead`, `Win′`, `bleach`) and **R3-R1-1 #2** (gauge, phase-dependent at pc 0);
- **(c) objects to R3-R1-3 #2**: its phase invariant `L->top = func+16 ∨ L->top = ci->top` is false after `CALL print` with A > 0 and C = 1, where `L->top = ra` (N2);
- **(c) objects to the costed edits in R3-R1-1 #2 R2.2** (`regauge`, 80–150 lines) **and R3-R1-5 #2** (the `LuaStateAt` "any value" rewrite plus `Complement.dead_congr`).

### The idea

`Complement` is about the snapshot `w.mo`, which is frozen for the life of `w`. Live memory meets it only through `Core.frame`, and only outside `Win` (N3). So:

- enlarge the window with the dead words;
- keep `w.mo` stale there, holding the boot values;
- leave `LuaStateAt.top : L->top = func+16` **true of `w.mo` forever**, and simply stop claiming it of live memory.

The complement never observes the new values, so nothing about it needs re-proving. The only price is at the *consumers*: lemmas that read live memory through `frame` at a dead address. Lua's own source names exactly one consumer class: `isIT` heads (N1).

```
Win′ p w a := Win p w a
            ∨ a ∈ [w.ci + 32, +8)                  -- ci->u.l.savedpc
            ∨ a ∈ [w.L  + 16, +8)                  -- L->top
            ∨ a ∈ [w.sp − Kc, w.sp)                -- callee frames (pure/tree helpers)
Core.frame   : ∀ a, ¬ Win′ p w a → bytesT1 mem a = bytesT1 w.mo a
Core.itTop   : isIT (p.fetch s.pc) → bytesT8 mem (w.L + 16) = BitVec.ofNat 64 (w.base + 16·nIn s)
```

In F1, `Core.itTop` has exactly one instance: VARARGPREP at pc 0, with `nIn = 0`, where `L->top = func + 16`. `vmRel_entry` gets it from the same `LuaStateAt` it already uses. The clause is keyed by `isIT`, not by "pc = 0", so it keeps working when the fragment admits `CALL B=0`, `RETURN B=0` or `VARARG`. At that point `L->top` becomes a VM-state component (the "open top") rather than a gauge. The key is correct because it is Lua's own.

### Core rules

- **L-C6′, non-slot part: `Core.frame` over `Win′`.** This is a definition, not a rule. It turns R3-R1-5 #2's `bleach` frame premise into `∀ a, ¬ Win′ a → ¬ Slots(defs) a → mem' a = mem a`. The `Protect` stores and callee frames satisfy it vacuously.
- **The gauge-fix rule (R3-R1-1 R2.3) becomes redundant.** A callee that reads `L->top` or `savedpc` reads what the *arm* wrote a few instructions earlier, and the executor carries that value symbolically. No relation fact is involved.
- **One new `Ranges` fact family: `gauge_out`.** It says code, `k`, the `ci+40` trap word, `.text` and `.rodata` are disjoint from the three new regions. The fetch, kconst, trap and rodata readers need it. The `ci` offsets are disjoint by `omega` (0, 32, 40). Code and `k` against the `L`/`ci` chunks come from `HeapAt`'s chunk disjointness at entry. `[sp−Kc, sp)` against the heap needs `brk_max + Kc ≤ sp`.

### What it makes free

- **(d1)** The `savedpc`/`L->top` widening goes from the forecast 100–250 lines to about 30, and nothing else in `Rel.lean` changes. There is no `LuaStateAt` edit, no `regauge`, no `dead_congr`, and no phase disjunction to maintain after CALL.
- **The callee-frame half of L-C6′** for every pure or tree helper: `luaV_equalobj`'s 48 bytes, and later `l_strcmp` and `luaH_getshortstr`.
- **The CALL follow-up.** `L->top = ra` after `poscall` (N2) needs nothing: `L->top` is in `Win′`.

### What it costs

- **Setup:** about 30 lines. That is `Win′`, `gauge_out` (4–5 fields), their discharge in `vmRel_entry`, `Core.itTop`, and re-plumbing `Win` to `Win′` in `Win.above`/`Core.*_of`.
- **What gets harder:**
  - `Kc` must bound the deepest helper frame on the normal paths that `sim_X` uses. The runtime callee (`print`) goes far deeper than `Kc`, and its contract must state its own stack use.
  - The relation now says *less* about live `L`/`ci`. The CALL contract, whose pre-state needs a live `LuaStateAt`, must be stated relative to the value the CALL arm writes (`ra + b`). The arm writes it anyway, so this matches L-C3′'s runtime shape.
- **Risk:** a hidden reader of `savedpc` with `trap = 0`. None exists on non-error paths in `lvm.c`/`ldo.c`, as far as I read. Falsifier F1 below checks it without reading anything.

### Theories

- **Known (Lua's own source as specification).** `lvm.c:1180` is a debug-build *havoc* of `L->top` at non-IT heads. Adopting a system's internal consistency assertions as the proof's frame conditions is runtime-assertion-as-specification (Hoare, "Assertions: a personal perspective", IEEE Annals 2003; recalled, check).
- **Known, distant field (operations engineering): chaos engineering.** A system property is established by injecting faults into the running system and checking that observables are unchanged (Basiri et al., "Chaos Engineering", IEEE Software 33(3), 2016; recalled, check). F1 below does exactly this to the dead words.
- **Known, distant field (hardware test):** memory poisoning and undefined-value tracking (Seward & Nethercote, "Using Valgrind to detect undefined value errors with bit-precision", USENIX ATC 2005; recalled, check).
- **Known (databases): snapshot isolation.** A reader holds a snapshot that may go stale on keys it does not read, and correctness needs only that it does not read them (Berenson et al., "A critique of ANSI SQL isolation levels", SIGMOD 1995; recalled, check). `w.mo` is a snapshot, and `Win′` is the set of keys it may not answer for.
- **Novel:**
  - a gauge set keyed by the *next instruction's* opmode bit, taken verbatim from the interpreter's debug assertion;
  - the observation that a frozen complement makes gauge invariance free, where round 1 priced it at 80–150 lines.

### Pilot

- **Smallest build:** `Win′`, `gauge_out`, `Core.itTop`, and `bleach` re-stated over `Win′` (M1).
- **MOD:** both edges carry the two `Protect` stores. `bleach`'s frame premise holds because both addresses are in `Win′`. **Measure:** 0 lines in `Rel.lean` beyond the setup, and no new `VmRel` field.
- **EQ:** the `Protect` pair plus `luaV_equalobj`'s frame at `[sp−48, sp)` are in `Win′`. The trap reload `lw t6,40(s7)` goes through `gauge_out` to `Complement.trap_word`.
- **MUL:** a control. `__muldi3` touches no memory (N4).
- **`sim_ADD`:** a control. It must not rise.

### Seed

`f0f`: frame · 0 · frame. The debug build writes `L->top := base` at offset 0 at every head, and the frame on both sides is unchanged by it. The zero write is invisible.

## 2. Constant-pool provenance: canonical values without an interning invariant

**Acts on:**
- **(c) objects to R3-R1-5 #1 `decode_canon`**, whose "short strings are unique per content" is a heap-wide invariant that CALL's interning must preserve. It also objects to **R3-R1-2 #1**'s fix of carrying a string-table map in the view, and to the interning invariant flagged by **R3-R1-2 #2, R3-R1-3 #4 and R3-R1-4 #2**. In F1, no register string ever comes from the string table.
- **(d)** Nobody in round 1 used the constant pool as the identity authority.

### The idea

In F1, every string in a register was copied, via LOADK then MOVE, from `p.k`. No F1 opcode makes a new string. GETTABUP yields only `print`. CALL's results are nil. `print`'s own `tostring` strings live above `L->top`, in killed slots. And `p.k` has no two constants with equal content: `addk` deduplicates by content (N6), and it can be a decidable `Supported` premise for protos that the project's own compiler emits.

So replace "strings are interned" with a **provenance clause** in the representation:

```
CanonRepr p w : BitVec 8 → BitVec 64 → Value → Prop
  | nil        : t = 0                                       -- N7: VNIL only
  | false_/true_/int/print   (as ValRepr)
  | str {i}    : kval p i = some (.str s) → t = slotTag w.mo (kslot i) → x = slotVal w.mo (kslot i)
```

Then one lemma, proved once, from `KDistinct p` and the pinned tags:

```
sig_eq_iff : CanonRepr v₁ (t₁,x₁) → CanonRepr v₂ (t₂,x₂) →
             (Sig t₁ x₁ = Sig t₂ x₂ ↔ v₁ = v₂)
Sig t x := (t &&& 63, if t &&& 63 ∈ {3, 4, 20, 22} then x else 0)
```

`Sig` is exactly what `luaV_equalobj` compares on the F1 tags (N5):
- different `tt & 63` gives 0;
- for nil, false and true it gives 1;
- for int it compares payloads;
- for shrstr it compares pointers;
- for lcf it compares payloads;
- for lngstr, `a == b` short-circuits to 1.

Different long-string pointers of equal length would still run `memcmp`. Either exclude them in `Supported`, or give `memcmp` a loop schema later.

**Side effect.** `decode` of a register becomes **heap-free**: a string decodes by a finite lookup of its pointer in `p.k`. So:
- the lens of R3-R1-1 #1 / R3-R1-2 #1 becomes *very well-behaved*, which was R3-R1-2 #1's named biggest risk;
- R3-R1-4 #1's "`dec`'s support includes live string bodies" disappears.

### Core rules

- **L-C4, value half: `CanonRepr` replaces `ValRepr` in `Core.stack`.** Preservation is free for every F1 arm. The value written is an int, a bool, nil with tag 0, or a 16-byte copy of a slot or K entry already satisfying `CanonRepr`.
- **`sig_eq_iff`.** One lemma replaces the whole "interning / equality-reflection" gap, for EQ, EQK and every later equality test.
- **`KDistinct p` is a `Supported` conjunct**, decided by `decide` on the proto.

### What it makes free

- **Two of the coordinator's three soundness gaps**, with one relation change: nil needs a canonical tag, and short strings need interning. The strengthening costs nothing in the proved arms: no proved arm writes a nil that is not tag 0, since MOVE and TESTSET copy tags.
- The heap/string-table widening that EQ would otherwise force before CALL is proved.
- Guard lemmas (M3): `CanonRepr` is functional in `(t, x) ↦ v`, so each tag test is one `cases`.

### What it costs

- **Setup:** about 50 lines. That is `CanonRepr`, `sig_eq_iff`, `KDistinct` and its `Supported` hookup, and `Complement.kconst` restated with `CanonRepr` (the K entries themselves are canonical by definition).
- **What gets harder:**
  - The provenance clause is **F1-specific**. When CONCAT or `tostring` results reach registers (F4), strings are born in the heap, and the real interning invariant returns. At that point this idea degrades into R3-R1-5 #1, having bought F1 for about 50 lines.
  - Future string producers must be listed.
- **Check first:** `Supported`'s program families must not contain two equal-length long-string constants that are compared with each other.

### Theories

- **Known (programming languages): hash-consing.** Maximal sharing makes structural equality coincide with pointer equality (Goto, "Monocopy and associative algorithms in an extended Lisp", 1974; Filliâtre & Conchon, "Type-safe modular hash-consing", ML Workshop 2006; both recalled, check). Here the constant pool is the hash-cons table and the compiler's `addk` is the consing function.
- **Known, distant field (biological nomenclature): name-bearing type specimens.** The identity of a species name is fixed by a pointer to one physical specimen, not by comparing descriptions (the ICZN, 4th ed., 1999, Arts. 72–75; recalled, check). A register string "is" `K[i]` because its pointer is `K[i]`'s specimen.
- **Known, distant field (archival science): chain of custody.** Provenance, rather than content inspection, certifies identity.
- **Novel:** discharging `luaV_equalobj`'s pointer equality by *provenance to a deduplicated constant pool* plus a decidable proto premise, instead of by a heap interning invariant.

### Pilot

- **Smallest build:** `CanonRepr` for nil, bool, int and str; `sig_eq_iff`; `KDistinct`.
- **EQ:** every leaf of the inlined helper tree (idea 5) closes with `sig_eq_iff`.
- **MUL / MOD / `sim_ADD`:** controls, since they involve ints only. `sim_ADD` must not rise.
- **Pre-check:** falsifier F3.

### Seed

`IqE`, the only exact repeat, at 30 and 107. The same content at two places is the same object, which is exactly what `addk` guarantees for constants.

## 3. The cheapest merge: one square, closed by `bleach`, with a two-mode call node

**Acts on:** **(b) combines**
- R3-R1-5 #1 (the camera square) with R3-R1-3 #5 (`kstep_iff`, forward evaluation), as M4;
- R3-R1-5 #2 (`bleach`), as M1, over idea 1's `Win′`;
- R3-R1-5 #1 `decode_tag` over idea 2's `CanonRepr`, as M3;
- R3-R1-4 #2 (abstract-`f` call leaf plus residual row), as M5;
- the vacuous-path lemma, as M6.

It also **(c) objects to R3-R1-2 #5 (`KProd`) as the carrier.** A product executor with kernel positions is 600–900 lines to rebuild what `kstep` already computes. Once guards are `decode_tag` rewrites, the kernel side is a `simp` normal form at each leaf, and no lockstep is needed.

### The idea

Each arm is proved by **one theorem, `square`**:

> For each machine path from `ArmAt` to the head with path condition π, suppose:
> - `kstep (dec c) = none` is derivable from π (vacuous), **or**
> - `dec c'` agrees on the def ports with `kstep (dec c)`, and `bleach`'s frame premise holds over `Win′`.
>
> Then `sim_X` holds.

The leaf closer is one tactic, `leaf_close`, the same for every opcode:

```
rw [kstep_iff]; simp only [opKernel, <combinator>, Opnd.fill, δ, decode_tag, sig_eq_iff, π]
exact bleach …
```

The call node has **two modes**, chosen by a row table:
- **summary** for loop helpers: `jal h` becomes `a0 := f args` with `mem' = mem`, per idea 4;
- **inline** for tree helpers: execution simply continues into the callee, per idea 5.

**Carrier.** The unmerged SymExec branch. The table shows how the combination fixes its three losses.

| SymExec loss | fix |
|---|---|
| 2–4× hand lines | Hand lines went into matching path conditions to kernel cases and final memory to `ValRepr`. The first is now `decode_tag` + `simp`, the second `bleach`. Both are opcode-independent. |
| no call nodes | Summary and inline modes. Their soundness is proved once: summary from a region spec (idea 4), inline trivially, because it is just more execution with the frame in `Win′`. |
| weaker theorem | The conclusion is `VmRel` itself. This assumes `2332c06` made `VmRelZ = VmRel`, as three round-1 agents claim; I have not checked it, and it needs checking at the first build. |

### Core rules

- **L-C4 → `square`.**
- **L-C6′ → `bleach` over `Win′`.**
- **L-C3′ pure → the summary node.** The inline mode needs no rule.
- **The L-C4 branch guard → `decode_tag`.**
- **(a5) inversions → `kstep_iff`**, which already exists.

## 4. The libgcc integer block as one multi-entry, memory-free region

**Acts on:**
- **(c) objects to R3-R1-5 #3**'s scanner, which walks "the helper's static call closure" by function symbol. N4 shows symbols do not delimit code here:
  - `__divdi3` has no `ret` of its own and falls into `__hidden___udivdi3`;
  - its sign paths live inside `__umoddi3`'s symbol range;
  - `__divsi3` jumps to `__moddi3+0x30`.

  A per-symbol scan or spec is ill-defined.
- **(c) sharpens R3-R1-2 #4**, which composes `__moddi3` through the `udivdi3` witness at "2 helpers × ~20 lines". That is right in spirit, but the composition must be over *entry points into one code region*, not over functions.
- **(a) extends R3-R1-1 #3 R3.2** (relocation by content). Relocate the *region* once, not per helper. The coordinator confirmed the bytes are identical to the WHILE build's after address normalisation.

### The idea

One spec for the whole block `B = [0x8002f6c8, 0x8002f7ec)` with an entry table:

```
RegionSpec B [(0x8002f6c8, BitVec.mul), (0x8002f72c, BitVec.sdiv), (0x8002f734, BitVec.udiv),
              (0x8002f77c, BitVec.umod), (0x8002f7b0, BitVec.srem)] :
  ∀ e F, (e,F) ∈ table → pc = e → ra = r → a1 ≠ 0 (div/mod entries) →
  ∃ n c', StepsN n c c' ∧ c'.pc = r ∧ a0' = F a0 a1
        ∧ mem' = mem                                  -- N4: no stores at all
        ∧ ∀ x ∉ {a0,a1,a2,a3,t0,ra}, reg' x = reg x
```

This is **stronger than L-C3′ pure.**
- Memory is *identical*, not "unchanged at or above `sp`".
- So MUL and MOD's summary nodes need no callee-frame reasoning at all, and `Kc` in idea 1 is driven only by the tree helpers.

The proof has three parts:
- two loop invariants (shift-add; restoring division), proved once each;
- straight-line glue per entry (the sign negations), by symbolic execution inside the region;
- the "closed region" side condition: all control targets inside `B`, and no `auipc`, load or store. I checked it by a scan (N4). It is decidable on the bytes, so it can be checked in Lean by `decide` over the byte list.

Relocation then works per region. If the WHILE proofs already exist for two of the entries, R3.2 transports them. Otherwise the proof is done here once, at this address.

### Core rules

- **L-C3′ pure (soft-int) → `RegionSpec` + `call_summary`.** There is one rule for any entry of any closed memory-free region. The table's `F` column is BitVec library functions, not δ, and the residual matching to δ is the arm's row (M5).
- **L-C2′ for helpers:** MUL/MULK share an entry; MOD/MODK share one; IDIV/IDIVK share `__divdi3`.

### What it makes free

- (b) for all 316 soft-int activations in the census: 4 helpers, 6 opcodes. There are **no per-symbol specs**, and `__divdi3`/`__moddi3`'s missing specs ("sites only") come out of the same region proof.
- The below-`sp` frame for these arms: there is none.

### What it costs

- **Setup:** two loop invariants (≈ 40–60 lines each, textbook), the entry glue (≈ 5–10 per entry, mostly `BitVec` sign lemmas: `srem` = `neg (umod (neg x) y)` and so on), and the region-closure scan (≈ 30).
- **What gets harder:**
  - The `udivdi3` divisor-by-zero leaf (`li a0,-1; beqz a2 → ret`) is reachable only when the arm's guard failed. MOD's `n ∈ {0,−1}` test (`bgeu 1, n+1`) excludes it, so the spec takes `a1 ≠ 0`.
  - `__divdi3` with `minint / −1` wraps. IDIV's arm also special-cases −1, so the spec can take `a1 ≠ −1` there.

### Theories

- **Known (programming languages): multiple-entry subroutines.** FORTRAN 77's `ENTRY` statement (ANSI X3.9-1978; recalled, check) gives several entry points into one body with shared tails. Specs are per entry over one body.
- **Known, distant field (molecular biology): overlapping genes.** One DNA region is read from several start codons and yields different proteins, as in bacteriophage φX174 (Barrell, Air & Hutchison, *Nature* 264, 1976; recalled, check). Annotating per "gene name" fails; you annotate the region with its entry table.
- **Known:** CompCert's treatment of compiler-support helpers (`__i64_*`) as axiomatised externals (Leroy; recalled, check). Here they are proved per region instead.
- **Novel:** the "closed memory-free region" side condition as a decidable byte property that upgrades pure to *identity-on-memory*, and region-level relocation.

### Pilot

- **Smallest build:** the region-closure check, the shift-add invariant and the `__muldi3` entry. That is enough for MUL.
- Then the restoring-division invariant and the `__moddi3` entry for MOD.
- EQ and `sim_ADD` are unaffected.
- **Measure:**
  - lines for the whole region against the per-helper forecast;
  - MUL and MOD's per-arm lines, which should show *zero* callee-frame content.

### Seed

`z32z`: a wrapper `z … z` around a digit core, as `__moddi3` is `neg … neg` around `udivdi3`. The non-monotone digit clusters `32 54 15 62 94 20` are entry offsets that jump around inside one block rather than following symbol order.

## 5. Inline tree helpers under the caller's canonical tags: no contract for `luaV_equalobj`

**Acts on:**
- **(c) objects to R3-R1-5 #3**, which classifies `luaV_equalobj` as class RO. By the scanner's own rules ("`jalr` inside a closure forces class U"), the switch's `jr a5` (N5) makes it U. Its static closure also contains `luaT_gettm` and `luaT_callTMres`, which are metamethod calls into Lua, so even a perfect static scan says runtime.
- **(c)** also objects to **R3-R1-1 #3 R3.2** for this helper: it has an `auipc` into `.rodata`, so it is not position-independent.
- **(c)** objects to **R3-R1-4 #4**'s classification of EQ's `valEq` as a *loss*. Once the helper is inlined, its `xor; andi 63` tag tests are ordinary speciation nodes.
- **(a) extends R3-R1-3 #4 and R3-R1-5 #3's "prove the helper with the arm's own machinery"** to its degenerate, cheapest form: do not prove the helper separately at all.

### The idea

A helper whose F1-pruned body is a finite tree needs no contract, no row and no residual. Such helpers are `luaV_equalobj`, `luaH_getshortstr` on a fixed key, and `luaV_tointeger` on an int. The executor simply **continues into it**:

- the `jal` pushes nothing to prove;
- the callee's `sd ra,40(sp)` / `ld ra,40(sp)` land in `[sp−Kc, sp)` ⊆ `Win′` (idea 1), and the executor tracks them;
- `jr a5` through the `.rodata` switch table resolves, because the index `tt & 63` is concrete on each path and `Complement.rodata` gives the table words. This is the same mechanism the incumbent uses for the dispatch `jr` through the opcode jump table.

**Pruning is by the caller's representation, not by a static scan.**
- At the call, `Core.stack` with `CanonRepr` (idea 2) says each operand's tag is in `{0, 1, 17, 3, 68, 84, 22}`.
- Every helper path that needs another tag is dead by `decode_tag`: the metamethod paths (tables, userdata) and the float path (19).
- On F1 tags the pruned `luaV_equalobj` has 5 leaves, and each is `a0 = [Sig A = Sig B]`:
  - different `tt & 63`, giving 0;
  - nil / false / true, giving 1;
  - int, a payload compare;
  - shrstr, a pointer compare;
  - lcf, a payload compare.
  - lngstr takes `a == b`, or is excluded by `Supported`; see idea 2.
- With EQ's k-bit test (`srliw 15; andi 1; bne a5,a0`), that gives 10 arm leaves. Each closes by `leaf_close` + `sig_eq_iff` against `docondjump`'s `if (!c.isFalse) = k then 1 else 0`.

**When to summarise instead:** only when the helper has a loop (the libgcc block, `memcmp`, `strlen`, `l_strcmp`) or is runtime-shaped (`print`). That cuts L-C3′ pure in two:
- loop helpers get *summaries*: a region spec (idea 4) or a loop schema;
- tree helpers get *inlining*, which costs no proof.

### Core rules

- **L-C3′ pure, tree-shaped → no rule.** Execution continues, and soundness is the executor's ordinary step soundness.
- **L-C6′ callee frame → `Win′`** (idea 1).
- **L-C4 branch guards inside the helper → `decode_tag`**, reused unchanged.

### What it makes free

- (b) for `luaV_equalobj` (EQ, EQK) and `luaH_getshortstr` (GETTABUP, 48 activations, read-only): no contract, no row value function, and no "reads through argument pointers" extension of the call node. R3-R1-4 #2 left that extension as a pilot measurement.
- The EQ-specific question "is `luaV_equalobj`'s `F` δ `.eq` modulo interning?" is dissolved into `sig_eq_iff` at the leaves.

### What it costs

- More paths in the executor: EQ goes from 2 to about 10 leaves. There is a CPU and memory cost and no hand cost.
- The executor must accept a `jr` whose target comes from a `.rodata` word read on a concrete index. It already does this at the head.
- **Risk:** the helper's compiled tree under F1 tags is larger than it looks. For example, `luaS_eqlngstr`'s call on long strings forces summary mode unless excluded. The executor's path count is the falsifier; see F4.

### Theories

- **Known (interprocedural analysis): the call-string approach against functional summaries.** Sharir & Pnueli, "Two approaches to interprocedural data flow analysis", 1981 (recalled, check). Inlining is call-strings of length 1, and is the precise choice when the callee is a finite tree.
- **Known, distant field (chemistry): one-pot, telescoped synthesis.** You do not isolate and characterise an intermediate when you can carry the reaction straight through (Hayashi, "Pot economy and one-pot synthesis", *Chem. Sci.* 7, 2016; recalled, check). A per-helper contract is an isolated intermediate; inlining telescopes it.
- **Novel:** pruning a callee by the *caller's* canonical representation invariant, so that a statically runtime-shaped helper (metamethod calls, indirect jumps) is contract-free in the fragment.

### Pilot

- **Smallest build:** inline mode (no new rule), `Win′`'s `[sp−Kc, sp)` with `Kc = 48`, and `CanonRepr` for the F1 tags.
- **EQ:** the whole proof is the row `(docondjump .eq, inline)` plus `leaf_close`. **Target: ≤ 8 hand lines.**
- **MUL / MOD:** not applicable. They use summary mode (idea 4).
- **`sim_ADD`:** a control.

### Seed

- `rjr`: the letters of `jr` framed by `r`. It is the indirect jump in the middle of the helper, resolved by what surrounds it.
- `kSk`: the k bit on both sides of the switch. EQ's `k = 0` and `k = 1` leaves are mirror images closed by one lemma.
