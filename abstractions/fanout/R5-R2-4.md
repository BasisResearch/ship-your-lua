## 1. Join invariants: prove the suffix after a confluence once

**Acts on:**
- (c) the tree members R5-R1-1 #3 (AnswerTree), R5-R1-2 #2 (reconciled fork trees) and R5-R1-4 #B (`ForkTree`, which has no join constructor);
- (c) the per-class goals of the view members R5-R1-1 #5, R5-R1-4 #C and R5-R1-5 #5;
- (a) extends R5-R1-5 #1's φ nodes from one FORLOOP observation to a general rule.

### 1. Name
**Confluence** (`Lua/Vm/Sim/Join.lean`): a named join assertion at every
machine pc where float paths meet. Each predecessor proves the assertion, and
the suffix is proved once from it.

### 2. The idea
Every round-1 arm proposal enumerates leaves:
- machine leaves (a tree, R5-R1-1 #3, R5-R1-4 #B);
- kernel classes (R5-R1-1 #5, R5-R1-4 #C, R5-R1-5 #5: "one goal per class").

The machine does not branch like that. GCC merges the float classes **before**
the expensive part.

- **SUB.** Three classes (flt/flt, flt/int, int/flt) meet at `0x8001e4ec/e4f0`
  and share one `jal __subdf3` at `e4f8` and one tag-store tail. A per-class
  proof runs the `__subdf3` call node and the tail 3 times. A per-leaf tree
  proof does the same, because a tree has no join.
- **FORPREP.** Three `tonumber` diamonds rejoin at `c13c`, `c154` and `c16c`,
  each through a stack slot. A product of operand classes
  (`{flt, int, str}³`, minus the int loop) has up to 26 combinations. The
  machine has 3 diamonds and then **one** continuation. The string case sits
  wholly inside the `luaV_tonumber_` callee, so it is not a class of the arm.
  This removes the "sixth attachment kind" worry in R5-R1-4 #C and the
  `tonumber`-failure classes of R5-R1-1 #5.
- **FORLOOP.** The φ at `e194` makes the store tail `e1a0–e1b8` a single
  suffix. A tree would duplicate it: R5-R1-2 #2 counts "four leaves", but the
  machine has two.

**The rule is weakest-precondition composition with the intermediate assertion
named.** `wp (diamond ; rest) Φ` holds if both sides of the diamond establish
`J`, and `J` implies `wp rest Φ`. Here `J` is a `structure … : Prop` listing
only the live locations. Examples:
- for SUB, `a0 = bits (tonum B)` and `a1 = bits (tonum C)`;
- for FORPREP's first diamond, `slot (sp+40) = bits (tonum limit)` plus the
  frame;
- for a φ, `a5 = b2n (guard o₁ o₂)`.

Without the name, `simp`/`at_run` unfolds `wp rest Φ` into both sides. That is
exactly the replay the census measured.

### 3. Core rules

| law | rule | decided at a use site by |
|---|---|---|
| L5-fork (refined: the machine merges as well as forks) | **`wp_join`**: `(∀ σ, J σ → Wp.run rest σ Φ) → Wp.run left σ₁ J → Wp.run right σ₂ J → …`. It is stated once over `MachWP` (`Wp.run` and `wp_segW` exist). | **syntactic.** A join pc is a node with in-degree > 1 in the arm's path DAG, which `float_paths.py` already enumerates and the generator emits as data. `J`'s fields are read off the live-in set at that pc. |
| L5-read′ (per operand, not per class) | **`NumView r x`**: `tonumber? (regs r) = some (.flt x n)`, with the conversion in a named field. It is produced by the diamond's two sides: the tag-19 side from `ValRepr.flt`, and the call side from `luaV_tonumber_`'s summary or `__floatdidf`'s spec. | syntactic. The diamond's side is fixed by the tag byte, which is a closed literal after `ValRepr` inversion. |
| frame across a join | `J` carries only the listed locations. Everything else is quantified away by `FrameMeta.memFrame_of_chain`/`abiFrame_of_wrChain`. | one `decide` on the closed write-log offsets (existing) |

**Symbolic data.** `J` mentions `bits (tonum v)` and atoms as opaque terms.
Nothing is cased at a join. Cases happen only at the next fork (§2).

**Search.** None is new. Choosing `J` is data. If a `J` is too weak, the suffix
fails with a named goal, which is the useful obstruction.

### 4. What it makes free
- **The shared-suffix replay**, measured at 8.3k heartbeats in FORLOOP.
  - Today it is quadratic in practice: (classes × suffix).
  - With joins it is linear: (Σ predecessor segments + one suffix).
- **SUB/SUBK/ADD/ADDK/MUL/MULK/DIV/DIVK/POW/POWK** (the ADDI-like and
  all-float fields): one call node per arm, not one per class.
- **FORPREP's coercions.** The product of operand classes becomes a sum: 3
  diamonds, one continuation.
- **Budget.** Each `wp rest` from `J` can be its **own declaration**: a lemma
  "from `J` at pc `X` the arm closes". This splits an arm across declarations
  without raising `maxHeartbeats`. It answers the forecast that SHL/SHR and
  LT/LE exceed 200k.

### 5. What it costs
- `wp_join` itself is about 20 lines over `MachWP`.
- One `J` structure per join site. That is data per site, not a proof per arm,
  but it is a new artefact for the generator to emit (pc, live locations,
  values).
- **Harder:** the two sides leave different dead state.
  - `luaV_tonumber_` clobbers caller-saved registers that the inline side
    keeps.
  - `ra` differs.
  - `J` must therefore list only callee-saved registers and the slot. The
    live-in set has to come from a liveness pass over the ELF, which
    `float_paths.py` does not do today.
- **Breaks if** GCC ever merges a join through a register that is dead on one
  side and live on the other. Then `J` needs a disjunction, which is
  forbidden. None of the three arms I read does this.

### 6. Theories
- **SSA φ-functions and dominance frontiers** (known: Cytron, Ferrante,
  Rosen, Wegman, Zadeck, *Efficiently computing static single assignment
  form*, TOPLAS 13(4), 1991). A join assertion is a φ placed at the
  confluence.
- **State merging in symbolic execution** (known: Kuznetsov, Kinder, Bucur,
  Candea, *Efficient state merging in symbolic execution*, PLDI 2012). This
  is the same move, with the merge made exact by a named invariant.
- **Braided-river confluences in fluvial geomorphology** (novel as an
  analogy). Channels split and rejoin. Discharge downstream of a confluence is
  measured once, not once per upstream channel.

### 7. Pilot
- **SUB (held out).**
  - `J` at `0x8001e4f0`: `a0 = bits (tonum B)`, `a1 = bits (tonum C)`.
  - Three predecessor runs, each ≤ 1 `__floatdidf` call.
  - One suffix: `__subdf3` → `Subdf3.spec`, store tag 19, dispatch.
  - Target: the suffix run appears once in the proof term. Measure heartbeats
    against a per-class version.
- **FORPREP (held out).** Three diamond `J`s and one φ `J` at `c1b8` (§5).
- **LT (held out).** Joins between the `intfitsf` side (`__floatdidf` then
  compare) and the f2i side, if they rejoin. I did not check LT's joins; count
  them first.
- **Refactor FORLOOP.** `J` at `e194`. That gives 2 suffix runs instead of 4.
- **Smallest thing:** `wp_join` and FORLOOP's `J_e194`.

### 8. Seed
*crossline* (lines crossing at a point), *thoroughfaresome* (one road
everyone takes after the junction), *cyanurate* (a three-fold ring: FORPREP's
three diamonds closing into one path).

## 2. Oriented atoms, cased lazily: the symmetry group collapses to one swap

**Acts on:**
- (c) the symmetry members R5-R1-1 #2 (Orbits), R5-R1-3 #B (site group) and R5-R1-4 #A (twin laws);
- (c) R5-R1-1 #3's claim that FORPREP is "the search case's first instance";
- (b) combines the cheapest M-tree member, R5-R1-3 #A (`at_run_fork` plus `cases o`), with the cheapest M-idiom member, R5-R1-5 #2 (`relOf` by evaluation on 4 points).

### 1. Name
**Orient** (`Lua/Vm/Sim/Atom.lean`).

### 2. The idea
The group `⟨σ swap, ν negate, β polarity, τ sign-flip⟩` proposed by three
round-1 agents has one load-bearing generator.
- **`ν` and `β` cost nothing** once the idiom is *evaluated* on the 4 compare
  points (R5-R1-5 #2's `relOf`). Negating the code or flipping `beqz`/`bnez`
  changes the closed `Bool` function that `decide` computes. No
  equivariance lemma is ever applied.
- **`τ` does nothing on the machine side.** The coordinator's correction
  agrees: `Subdf3.F` is a different DAG.
- **Only `σ` remains.** Apply it **once per atom, at atomisation**. Orient
  every compare atom to a canonical pair order, the pair's first occurrence in
  the *machine* path. Rewrite each kernel `Float.Model.lt/le/beq x y` into a
  function of that one oriented atom by `compare_swap`.

After orientation, round 1's coupling worry disappears on the held-out arms.
- **FORPREP.** R5-R1-1 #3 predicted that `compare fs 0` and `compare 0 fs`
  would need a coupling search. On the ELF, both machine calls are on
  `(step, 0)` (`c17c`, `c198`). The only swap is the kernel's `lt 0 fs`,
  rewritten once.
- **The bound.** It uses `(limit, init)` on both φ sides (`c1ac`, `ecf4`).
- **Result.** FORPREP has **2 atoms and 16 valuations**, and `recon.py` agrees
  on all 16. FORLOOP is the same: `(step, 0)` and `(limit, idx′)`, 16/16.

**Lazy casing** (R5-R1-3 #A) beats a product `decide` (R5-R1-1 #3, R5-R1-4 #B,
R5-R1-5 #1).
- Atom `oᵢ` is `cases`d only at a fork whose guard mentions it, so the cost is
  per machine path, not `4^n`.
- No tree datatype, mesh, deep embedding or normaliser is built. The kernel is
  evaluated at each leaf under the partial valuation, by `decide`/`rfl` on
  literals.

### 3. Core rules

| law | rule | decided at a use site by |
|---|---|---|
| L5-idiom | **`idiom_eval`**: `ι.eval (R.S a b) = relOf R ι (F64.compare a b)`, where `relOf R ι o := ι.eval (code R o)`. It is `rfl` after unfolding `R.S`'s match (R5-R1-5 #2). | computation: `relOf` on a closed `ι` and a closed `o` is a literal `Bool` |
| L5-comm (compare part only) | **`orient`**: `F64.compare b a = (F64.compare a b).map Ordering.swap` (`compare_swap`), applied when `(b, a)` is not the canonical order | **syntactic:** the canonical order is first occurrence on the machine path, a closed list of operand terms |
| L5-fork | **`at_run_fork`** (R5-R1-3 #A2): at a fork whose guard is `ι.eval (R.S a b)`, rewrite by `idiom_eval` and `orient`, `generalize` the atom to `o`, then `cases o` (4 children, once per atom per path) | computation. No closer is tried, because the guard is a literal in each child |
| kernel side | the kernel's `Float.Model.lt/le/beq x y` is rewritten by `lt_eq_model`/`le_eq_model`/`eq_eq_model` (they exist) and `orient` into a function of the same atoms | `simp only` with a fixed lemma set, once per kernel combinator |

**Symbolic data.** Payloads are never cased. An atom's operands are opaque
terms. The integer atom `intfitsf i` is a `Bool`: `cases` on it, both sides
compute it.

**Search.** None, on the four pilot arms. A genuine coupling arises only when
the machine compares a pair that the kernel never mentions. §3 argues this
does not occur for the f2i chain. If it occurs elsewhere, the leaf fails with
a named `Bool` mismatch. That is the obstruction to report, not a reason to
build a filter.

### 4. What it makes free
- **The polarity trials** (10.3k in FORLOOP). Every fork is a literal.
- The 17 idioms at 93 sites (C5-idiom) need **no lemma at all**: no table
  `decide` (R5-R1-3 #A1, R5-R1-4 #A) and no 10 instruction-kind lemmas
  (R5-R1-2 #3). `relOf` evaluates.
- **O-ord.** GTI's `__gedf2(x, imm)`, FORLOOP's `add(step, idx)` (via
  `add_comm` at the value leaf, the only non-compare swap), FORPREP's
  `lt 0 fs`.
- **What is no longer needed:** the descriptor types, the 16-element group,
  `canon_spec`, and the site recogniser stated as a Lean function of
  instruction words (R5-R1-3 #B "harder").

### 5. What it costs
- `relOf`/`idiom_eval`: about 30 lines.
- `orient` as a `simp` procedure keyed by a closed operand order: about 30
  lines.
- `at_run_fork`: the one real kit change. The at-runner must stop at a fork
  with an opaque guard. This feasibility is unverified, because I may not read
  the kit.
- **Harder:** a spilled answer (7 sites) is carried in a slot before its
  fork. `generalize` must happen at the call's return, with the slot's value
  stated as `code R o` in a join `J` (§1).

### 6. Theories
- **Canonical orientation in computational geometry and graph
  canonisation** (known: e.g. orient each undirected edge by vertex index,
  McKay, *Practical graph isomorphism*, Congr. Numer. 30, 1981). One
  orientation per pair removes the swap symmetry before any search.
- **Lazy case splitting in DPLL(T)** (known: Nieuwenhuis, Oliveras, Tinelli,
  *Solving SAT and SAT modulo theories*, JACM 53(6), 2006). Split on an atom
  only when a clause, here a fork, needs it. This is a cited theory, not a
  solver as the abstraction.
- **Gauge fixing** (R5-R1-1 #2, R5-R1-3 #B) taken to its minimum: fix the gauge
  on the atoms, not on the sites (novel framing of the reduction).

### 7. Pilot
- **FORPREP.** Two atoms, `o₁ = compare step 0` and `o₂ = compare limit init`.
  The kernel clause `forprepK`'s float branch becomes a function of
  `(o₁, o₂)` by `orient`. Expected: no coupling, 16 leaf evaluations.
- **LT.** Atoms are:
  - `compare a b` (flt/flt);
  - `intfitsf i`;
  - `compare (ofI i) f`;
  - the chain's three (§3);
  - one integer compare.

  Lazy casing keeps the count per path.
- **SUB.** No compare atoms (control).
- **FORLOOP.** 2 atoms; the polarity trials disappear.
- **Smallest thing:** `relOf`, `orient`, and a closed `decide` in Lean of the
  FORPREP and FORLOOP reconciliations that `recon.py` checked.

### 8. Seed
*recenter* (re-centre each comparison on one canonical pair), *qualityless*
(an atom carries no data, only an orientation), *proventricular/preday* (digest
the orientation before the fork grinds).

## 3. The kernel already is the chain: no feasibility filter, no order closure, no layout certificates

**Acts on:**
- (c) R5-R1-3 #E (Galois rules plus a reflective order-closure checker);
- (c) R5-R1-3 #A's feasibility filter `Φ` and R5-R1-4 #B's `feas`;
- (c) R5-R1-5 #1's "the f2i fold is a must";
- (c) R5-R1-1 #4 (layout certificates) and R5-R1-4 #A's `ChainShape` (≈3k-case `decide`).

### 1. Name
**Shape-faithful kernel** (a discipline plus two lemmas; no new module).

### 2. The idea
Round 1 assumed that the kernel states f2i *semantically* (floor and ceil as
order facts) while the machine tests three dependent compares. Machine and
kernel would then agree only on *feasible* valuations, hence filters,
closures and folds.

**The repository's kernel is not written that way.**
- `Lua.Num.flttointeger` is `if !(beq n f) then … else numberToInteger f`.
- `numberToInteger` is `le minIntF f && lt f minIntF.neg`.
- That is lvm.c's control shape, and the ELF runs the same tests in the same
  order:
  - `__eqdf2(n, ⌊n⌋)`;
  - `__gedf2(f, −2^63)`, which is `le minIntF f` after `orient`;
  - `__ledf2(f, 2^63)`, which is `lt f (2^63)`;
  - then `__fixdfdi` (`0x8001f130–f180`).
- `ltIntFloat`/`leIntFloat` likewise mirror the `intfitsf` split.

So, after §2's orientation, machine and kernel are **the same Boolean function
of the same atoms**. They agree on *every* valuation, infeasible ones
included, by `decide` or by `rfl`.

Feasibility is needed only when one side tests an atom the other never
mentions. If GCC reordered the two range tests, they would still be the same
function, because `&&` of two atoms commutes as a `Bool` fact and needs no
order theory.

What remains is **data, at the one `some` leaf**: `__fixdfdi f` equals
`(toModel f).toInt64`. That needs the range, and the range is the two guard
atoms as hypotheses. It is one lemma. The `.ceil` mode's `f + 1` is a data term
`Adddf3.S f one` that both sides share.

### 3. Core rules

| law | rule | decided at a use site by |
|---|---|---|
| L5-chain | **none.** The chain is three more atoms in §2's run, and the layouts are invisible because polarity is inside `relOf` | computation (lazy `cases`, literal guards) |
| data at `some` | **`fixdfdi_inrange`**: `F64.compare f minF ∈ {gt, eq} → F64.compare f twoP63 = some .lt → Fixdfdi.S f = (toModel f).toInt64.toBitVec` (once) | rewrite. The hypotheses are the two atoms' literal values in that leaf |
| constants | `bytes 0x8005c478 = bits minIntF`, `bytes 0x8005c480 = bits minIntF.neg` | one ground `decide` each over `Lua/Vm/Image.lean` (`FixedBytesLoaded`) |
| floor | `FloorSpec`, the named premise every round-1 agent already wanted | premise |
| (new, enforcement) | **discipline rule:** a float kernel helper in `Lua/Num/Arith.lean` must keep lvm.c's test order and atoms. A semantic characterisation (R5-R1-3 #E1's Galois statements) is a theorem *about* the kernel, never its definition | a `scripts/discipline_rules.tsv` line plus review |

**Symbolic data.** `f`, `⌊n⌋` and `Fixdfdi.S f` are opaque. **Search:** none.

### 4. What it makes free
- **C5-f2i** (20 sites, 6 layouts, 13 fields) with **no chain summary**. That
  saves:
  - R5-R1-1 #4's `Cert`/`summary_of_iso` (≈150 lines plus the
    cross-jumping risk);
  - R5-R1-3 #E4's order-closure checker (≈100 lines plus a NaN-aware order
    with no ℚ);
  - R5-R1-4 #A's ≈3k-case shape `decide`;
  - R5-R1-5 #1's `norm` fold.
- **LT/LE's mixed classes, EQ/EQK's `eqNum`, the bitwise family's
  `tointegerns .eq`, and FORPREP's int-path `forLimit`:** all the same
  function of atoms on both sides.

### 5. What it costs
- `fixdfdi_inrange`: about 30 lines over `F64`'s `truncInt?`.
- Two ground `decide`s.
- **Lazy casing grows with the chain's depth.** LT's longest path has about 6
  atoms on one path, which gives ≤ 4·2·4·4·4·2 leaf evaluations in the worst
  case. Each is a literal-guard step, so the forecast is fine.
  - If not, a §1 join after the chain (it rejoins with `some n` / `none`)
    cuts it to "chain once, then the arm".
- **Breaks if** an arm's machine path tests a compare the kernel does not
  contain (e.g. a GCC-introduced `x == x` NaN pre-test). Then the
  `decide` fails at a named leaf. That is the moment to add a coupling, and
  not before.

### 6. Theories
- **Translation validation by structural correspondence** (known: Necula,
  *Translation validation for an optimizing compiler*, PLDI 2000). Keep the
  specification in the implementation's control shape, so validation is a
  syntactic match, not a semantic search.
- **Allophony versus phonemic contrast** (known in phonology: Trubetzkoy,
  *Grundzüge der Phonologie*, 1939). Two realisations need a rule only if
  they contrast. Here the kernel and machine never contrast on an infeasible
  valuation, because they are the same word.
- **Bisimulation up to the identity** (known: Sangiorgi, *On the bisimulation
  proof method*, MSCS 8(5), 1998). The cheapest relation is equality.

### 7. Pilot
- **LT (held out).** The decisive arm.
  - Measure that `ltIntFloat`'s `.ceil` branch closes by the same leaf
    `decide`s as the float/float class, with zero chain-specific lines.
- **FORPREP (held out).** Its int path's `forLimit` uses
  `tointegerns (floor|ceil)`. That is outside `ForCoerce`'s float clause, but
  the same claim applies.
- **SUB/FORLOOP.** No chain (control).
- **Cheapest falsifier:** a 64-point closed `decide` in Lean, comparing

## 4. Round-to-odd, minimal: five rules, margins stated at the machine's scale

**Acts on:**
- (b) merges the five M-odd members: R5-R1-1 #1 (`odd`), R5-R1-2 #1 (`ro`), R5-R1-3 #C (`StEq` congruence), R5-R1-4 #D (`Stamped`) and R5-R1-5 #3 (`rto`);
- (c) the margin side conditions in R5-R1-3 #C ("linear in `d` and `log2`, so `omega` can do it") and R5-R1-1 #1 `cancel_le_one` ("arithmetic, with `L` generalised (linear)").

### 1. Name
**RtoCore** (`Lua/Num/Rto.lean`).

### 2. The idea
**The five members are the same mechanism.** `StEq k n m` is
`rto n k = rto m k` plus a nonzero high part. `Stamped v X k m` is
`v = rto X k ∧ m ≥ 2`. The cheapest member is the **function form**. The
smallest rule set closing the open hand-off for `__adddf3`/`__subdf3` is:

1. **`srs_rto`**: the machine idiom equals `rto x k`, for `1 ≤ k ≤ 63`.
2. **`rto_add_even`** and **`rto_sub_even`**: `2 ∣ A → A ± rto B k = rto (A·2^k ± B) k` (sub needs `B ≤ A·2^k`).
3. **`shr_rto`**: `2 ≤ s → shr (rto n k) false false s = shr n false false (s + k)`. This is the innocuous double rounding.
4. **`rto_exact`**: `2^k ∣ n → rto n k = n / 2^k`.
5. **`log2_rto`**: `2^(k+1) ≤ n → log2 (rto n k) + k = log2 n`.

**Dropped as unnecessary:**
- `rto_rto` composition (R5-R1-1, R5-R1-4, R5-R1-5). For the carry
  renormalisation and the subnormal second SRS, apply `shr_rto` twice.
- The `StEq` relation and its closure rules.
- The `Stamped` record.

`rto.py` confirms the load-bearing preconditions:
- `shr_rto` fails at `s = 1` (`n = 3, k = 1`);
- the anchor law fails for odd `A` (`A = 1, k = 1, B = 1`);
- both hold in all tested cases with the stated preconditions.

**The objection.** The margin side condition is not linear as round 1 states
it. R5-R1-3 #C step 3 needs `log2 (A·2^d + B) ≥ 55 + d` from `A ≥ 2^55`. The
term `A·2^d` multiplies two non-literal atoms, so `omega` cannot use it.
R5-R1-1 #1's `cancel_le_one` has the same problem.

**The fix ("re-centring").** Never state a bound on the exact sum. State it on
the **machine-scale value** `v = A ± rto(B, d)`, whose bounds are literal:
- `A ∈ [2^55, 2^56)`, because it is semiraw with the hidden bit;
- `rto(B, d) ≤ 2^54` for `d ≥ 2` and `B < 2^56`;
- so `v ∈ [2^54, 2^57)`, and `log2 v ∈ {54, 55, 56}` by one literal-bounds
  lemma.

Transport to the exact sum through `log2_rto` (rule 5). The only fact about
`d` is then `4 ≤ 2^d` for `d ≥ 2`, which is linear once `2^d` is an atom.
- The cancellation bound `A − rto(B, d) ≥ 2^54` is checked: 0 violations in
  200k random semiraw pairs.
- It is *the machine's own normalisation guarantee*: at most one leading zero,
  so the jam bit sits ≤ 1 above bit 0, and the 3 work bits leave margin ≥ 2.

### 3. Core rules (decided how)

| law | rule | decided at a use site by |
|---|---|---|
| L5-stage (SRS) | `srs_rto` | syntactic match on the SRS node. `1 ≤ k ≤ 63` from the dominating guard (`bge 56, a6`, `blez a6`), by `omega` on literals |
| hand-off | `rto_add_even`/`rto_sub_even`, then `shr_rto` | rewrite. `2 ∣ A` from `A = f <<< 3` (literal shift, `omega`); `2 ≤ s` from the literal machine-scale bounds |
| margins | `log2_of_bounds : 2^a ≤ v → v < 2^(a+1) → log2 v = a` with literal `a`; `log2_rto` | rewrite plus `omega` on literal exponents. **No `A·2^d` is ever in a goal** |
| exact regimes `d ≤ 1` | `rto_exact` (low 3 bits zero) | `omega` on literals |
| round + pack | L5-stage's `roundPack = F64.round` stage lemma (checked on 7,280 cases), proved once | rewrite |

**Symbolic data.** `A`, `B`, `d` and `2^d` stay symbolic. Literals appear only
in bounds. **Search:** none.

### 4. What it makes free
- The finite × finite core of `__adddf3` and `__subdf3`, on all `d` regimes:
  - `d ≥ 57`: pure sticky, `rto` with `B < 2^k`;
  - `2 ≤ d ≤ 56`: the rules above;
  - `d ≤ 1`: exact.
- `__floatdidf`'s single SRS.
- **`__muldf3`** [recall: check `op-common.h`]: its product collapse is an SRS
  by a *constant*, so it fits.

### 5. What it costs
- About 120 lines of `Nat` lemmas. `shr_rto` is the deep one, about 50 lines.
- **Where it breaks:**
  - **`__divdf3`.** The sticky is `rem ≠ 0` against a divisor `D`, not
    against `2^k`. It needs `rtoQ N D = N / D ||| [N % D ≠ 0]` and a
    `shr_rtoQ`. That is a second lemma, out of pilot scope.
  - **Subnormal results.** The second SRS by `1 − e` is `shr_rto` again, with
    margin 3 at a coarser position. Fine, but `target`'s `max … (−1074)` split
    is a 2-way case per routine.
  - **NaN.** Untouched: special leaves close by `rfl` on the canonical NaN
    (L5-comm checked it bit for bit).
- **Still needed and shared with every member:** a stage cut that makes the
  SRS node and the `A ± ·` node visible. The pack and round joins already are
  named `N_<pc>` functions of their live-ins (e.g. the pack join that R5-R1-3
  #D5 cites). Use those as the cut before asking the generator for more.

### 6. Theories
- **Round to odd** (known: Boldo & Melquiond, IEEE TC 57(4), 2008). The
  `s ≥ 2` margin is theirs; `rto.py` confirms that `s = 1` fails on
  `F64.shr`.
- **Interval bookkeeping at a fixed scale, as in fixed-point DSP design**
  (known: block floating point, e.g. Oppenheim & Schafer, *Discrete-Time
  Signal Processing*, ch. on quantisation). Bounds are carried at the
  datapath's scale, where they are constants. Exact values are reached only
  through a scale map.
- **Dimensional analysis / non-dimensionalisation in physics** (known as a
  method; novel here). Re-centre to the scale where quantities are O(1)
  literals, and keep the scale factor as one symbol.

### 7. Pilot
- **`__adddf3` (held out).** Same sign, `2 ≤ d ≤ 56`. Then opposite sign,
  through the machine-scale cancellation bound. Metric: no goal containing
  `A * 2^d`, and no `omega` call over a variable shift.
- **`__subdf3` (held out).** Re-instantiate. The rules do not see signs.
  Metric: new lines beyond the stage match.
- **Smallest thing:** `rto`, `shr_rto`, `rto_sub_even`, `log2_of_bounds`, and
  the closed `decide`s mirroring `rto.py` on `n < 2^8` (both counterexamples
  included as `¬` statements, to pin the preconditions).

### 8. Seed
*twizzened, hydremia, subpotency* (a shrivelled, diluted residue that still
marks the whole), *recenter* (move the bookkeeping to the machine's scale),
*plethoretical* (the 3 work bits are an excess margin; the proof needs to
know only that 2 survive).

## 5. Sign-steered comparison: one kernel combinator and one machine rule for FORPREP and FORLOOP

**Acts on:**
- (d) a combinator none of round 1 named;
- (a) extends R5-R1-5 #1's observation of FORLOOP's φ to FORPREP, `forLimit` and `forCount`;
- (c) R5-R1-5 #4 and R5-R1-1 #5, which declare FOR* unshareable ("L5-share false").

### 1. Name
**`bySign`** (a combinator in `Lua/Bytecode/Semantics.lean`, through
`opKernel`'s combinator discipline; machine rule in `Lua/Vm/Sim/Join.lean`).

### 2. The idea
lvm.c has one recurring idiom: **the step's sign selects a relation (or a
mode), which is then applied to one pair**.
- `floatforloop`: `0 < step ? idx <= limit : limit <= idx`.
- float `forprep`: `0 < step ? limit < init : init < limit`.
- `forLimit`: `step < 0 ? F2Iceil : F2Ifloor`.
- `forCount`: `0 < step ? … : …`.

On the ELF, both float instances compile to the **same shape**:
- one call on `(step, 0)`, with one fork;
- each side calls a compare on the **same oriented pair** and turns it into a
  0/1 value with an idiom;
- a φ (`e194`, `c1b8`), then one `bnez`/`beqz`.

L5-share is false *against the int clause*. It is true **between FORPREP and
FORLOOP**, at this skeleton.

Define in the kernel:

```lean
def bySign (R : Rel) (st : Float.Model) (x y : Float.Model) : Bool :=
  if Float.Model.lt zero st then R x y else R y x
```

`forloopK` uses it with `le` on `(idx, l)`, and `forprepK` with `lt` on
`(l, i)`. This is one refactor of two kernel clauses. `kstep_iff` still
derives `Step`.

The machine rule is proved **once over a closed table** of
`(ι₊, R₊, ι₋, R₋, Rel)` rows:
- FORLOOP: `(not;srli 63, ge, slti 1, le, le)`;
- FORPREP: `(srli 63, le, sgtz, ge, lt)`.

Its content is "the φ value equals `bySign Rel` as a function of
`(o₁, o₂)`", by one `decide` over rows × 16. `recon.py` checks both rows.

### 3. Core rules

| law | rule | decided at a use site by |
|---|---|---|
| L5-fork + L5-idiom at a φ | **`bySign_phi`**: for each row of the closed table, the φ's 0/1 value is `b2n (bySignAtoms Rel o₁ o₂)` | **computation:** one `decide` over the table × `(Option Ordering)²`, proved once, never per arm |
| kernel side | **`bySign_atoms`**: `bySign Rel st x y = bySignAtoms Rel (compare st 0) (compare x y)` from the `_eq_model` bridges and `orient` | proved once |
| use in an arm | the φ's join `J` (§1) is `a5 = b2n (bySign Rel st x y)`. The suffix forks once on it | syntactic: the arm names its row |

**Symbolic data.** `st`, `x` and `y` are opaque. Two atoms; the row is a
literal. **Search:** none.

### 4. What it makes free
- **FORLOOP's bound test** (the 2×2 outcome split, 4 lines, and the polarity
  closers).
- **FORPREP's skip test.** It is the same rule with the other row.
- **`forLimit`/`forCount` (int FORPREP).** Their step-sign selection is
  `bySign` over an integer guard. That is a third row, if the int arm is ever
  refactored.
- What a FOR* arm then owns: its views, the step-zero error leaf (no `Step`),
  and its store tail.

### 5. What it costs
- `bySign` and the two kernel-clause edits: about 15 lines. The kernel is
  shared, so check that `Lua/Bytecode/Kernel.lean`'s derived facts still go
  through.
- `bySign_phi`: about 30 lines plus one `decide` over 2 × 16 points.
- **Harder:** the φ *value* on the ELF lives in different registers in
  different arms (`a5` in FORLOOP; `a4` in FORPREP at `c1b4`/`ed00`). The
  row must carry the register as data.
- **Breaks if** a future build makes GCC duplicate the tail instead of
  merging at the φ. Then the rule degrades to two forks on the same atom,
  still one `decide` per row.

### 6. Theories
- **Higher-order combinator extraction / anti-unification** (known: Plotkin,
  *A note on inductive generalization*, Machine Intelligence 5, 1970). The
  least general generalisation of the two kernel clauses is `bySign`.
- **Bimodal respiration in lungfish (Dipneusti)** (known in physiology; novel
  as an analogy). One organism with two breathing organs; the environment
  (water or air, here the step's sign) selects the organ, and the outcome
  (oxygen, here a `Bool`) is the same kind of thing.
- **Multiplexers in digital design** (known): a select line chooses between
  two data inputs that feed one consumer. The φ is a mux, and `bySign_phi` is
  its truth table.

### 7. Pilot
- **FORPREP (held out).** Views (§1 diamonds), then `bySign_phi` row FORPREP,
  then the store tail `c1c0–c1e8` once. Expected: no FORPREP-specific
  compare reasoning.
- **Refactor FORLOOP.** Row FORLOOP. Expected 29 lines → about 5:
  - views for 3 registers (R5-R1-2 #4's `readPorts`);
  - prefix to `e17c`;
  - `bySign_phi`;
  - `J_e194`;
  - one suffix.
- **LT/SUB.** Not touched (control).
- **Smallest thing:** `bySign`, the FORLOOP and FORPREP clause edits, and
  `bySign_phi` with its 32-point `decide`.

### 8. Seed
*Dipneusti* (two ways of breathing, chosen by the surroundings),
*crossline* (the two compare lines cross at the φ), *prostatocystotomy* (one
incision through two organs: one combinator through FORPREP and FORLOOP).
